Workflow rule: Subordinate offices submit to their designated controlling office. The controlling office reviews, returns corrections where required, verifies and compiles the records. ADG(B) compiles the five verified controlling-office streams together with its independent own-office stream. Draft and returned records are excluded from official totals; only verified/accepted records count.
