ADG(B) REPORT SUBMISSION PORTAL
Subject text readability update (index.html only)

===========================================================================
WHAT CHANGED
===========================================================================

The "Report subject" column previously ran the whole thing together as one
long bold sentence, e.g.:

  "Rationalization of the policy regarding recovery of wrongly excess
   payment made to Govt Servants for the Month of Aug,2026"

It's now split visually into two clearly separated parts:

  Rationalization of the policy regarding recovery of wrongly excess
  payment made to Govt Servants for Servants           <- bold, larger
  🗓 Monthly · Aug,2026                                  <- small pill below

The period is shown as a small rounded pill, colour-coded by schedule type
so you can tell report types apart at a glance:
  - Monthly            -> blue pill, 🗓
  - Quarterly (QE)      -> green pill, 📊
  - One-time            -> amber pill, 📌

This applies to both the desktop table and the mobile card view, and still
works correctly with the existing PDF-hyperlink feature (the underlined,
clickable portion of a subject name).

Nothing else changed -- your previous "Fix folder names now" button and
folder-naming fix in Code.gs are untouched; Code.gs was not modified this
round at all.

===========================================================================
DEPLOY
===========================================================================
1. Replace index.html in your GitHub Pages repo (ADG-B-Report-Portal) with
   the supplied index.html.
2. Commit the change (the green "Commit changes..." button on GitHub --
   this step is required, editing alone doesn't publish it).
3. Wait 30-60 seconds for GitHub Pages to rebuild, then hard refresh the
   portal (Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+R), or use the portal's own "Clear portal cache"
   button on the login screen.

No Apps Script / Code.gs changes or redeploy needed for this update.
