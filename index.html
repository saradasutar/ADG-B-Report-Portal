Automatic subject schedule

One-time: appears only during the selected month/year, then disappears.
Monthly only: opens automatically every month for the previous reporting month.
Monthly + Quarterly (QE): monthly continues, and the QE row also opens automatically on 1 January, 1 April, 1 July and 1 October for the quarter ended in the previous month.
Quarterly only (QE): no monthly requirement at all -- only appears in Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct for the QE quarter just ended.
Fortnightly: a new 14-day window opens automatically from the start date, continuing indefinitely unless an end date is set (e.g. roughly 3 months out for a temporary requirement).

Switching schedule here changes it for every office from now on. Past submissions and files are always kept.

Visibility: Hide from dashboard removes a subject for every dashboard user without deleting its history or files.
Auto-create per subject: One-time · Monthly only · Monthly + Quarterly (QE). For Monthly + Quarterly, the monthly row is created every month and the QE row is created automatically from the 1st day of the month following quarter end.
Disabled subjects remain visible and existing reports can still be viewed. Renaming preserves IDs, reports and linked PDFs.
📧 Pending report reminders — set each office's email address below, then send every office its own current Monthly/Quarterly pending list with one click. An office with nothing pending gets a short "all clear" email rather than being silently skipped.
To send these automatically on a schedule (no manual clicking): open the Apps Script editor for this portal → Triggers (clock icon, left sidebar) → Add Trigger → function scheduledPendingStatusEmailJob → Time-driven → pick how often (e.g. Day timer, 9am–10am) → Save. It always reflects whatever is genuinely pending at the moment it runs.
